DesignCrit Pro Research Study

You are invited to participate in a research study investigating whether automated design feedback can help beginner designers improve their work.

What you will do

During this study you will:

  1. Upload an original design created before receiving automated feedback.
  2. Review feedback generated by DesignCrit Pro.
  3. Improve your design using the feedback.
  4. Upload the revised version of the same design.
  5. Complete a short questionnaire about your experience.

Research data

Your design scores, improvement results and questionnaire responses will be stored for research analysis. A participant code will be used instead of your name.

Participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop before completing the study if you no longer wish to participate.

Do not create a completely different design.
